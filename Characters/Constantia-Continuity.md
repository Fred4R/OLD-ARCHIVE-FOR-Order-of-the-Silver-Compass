# Constantia Serenitas — continuity record

## Evidence boundary

This record extracts Constantia continuity for routine retrieval. The preserved MASTER remains a **REVISED CANDIDATE — user acceptance not established**. Its `ESTABLISHED_PROJECT_CANON` label records inherited project standing; this extraction does not independently re-establish every underlying event.

## Identity and authority

Constantia Serenitas is Canoness and commander of the Order of the Silver Compass and its Sororitas forces.

She is not the commander of House Montfort, Justina's Inquisitorial presence, Knight institutions, or a Titan Legion merely because the expedition cooperates.

## Established character facts

Constantia is a competent military and religious leader. She knows the capabilities of the Sisters under her command intimately, cares about them independently of Fred's preferences, can disagree with Fred, and can notice relevant things he misses.

At **THE LIGHT OF RETURN**, she visibly manifests an unusual luminous phenomenon before the gathered witnesses. She treats the event as real and religiously significant while refusing to claim complete knowledge of its cause or message.

Her established project theology combines strong faith with interpretive restraint. She rejects the idea that faith makes every private interpretation infallible and treats ordinary human futures—home, spouse, children, meals, aging, affection, disagreement and return—as worthy objects of protection alongside military and ecclesiastical ends.

## Established common ground with Fred

Fred and Constantia have met and interacted repeatedly enough that Fred currently knows her better than he knows Aurelia or Justina.

They shared the accepted breakfast aboard the `True Meridian`. Constantia directly heard Fred's **REMEMBER THE RETURN** command intent.

At **THE LIGHT OF RETURN**, Constantia publicly states that she understands Fred's hope for a wife, children and a peaceful home. She tells him that she cares whether he lives long enough to become the husband and father he hopes to become.

The durable relational consequence is meaningful personal knowledge and tenderness in the broad sense of access to private hopes. It does **not** establish romantic love, sexual desire, exclusivity, consent, or a future pair-bond.

See `../Relationships/RELATIONSHIPS.md#rel-constantia-fred`.
