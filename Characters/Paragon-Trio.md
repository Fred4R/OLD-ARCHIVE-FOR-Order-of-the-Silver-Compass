# Three Silver Compass Paragon pilots — continuity record

## Established collective identity

Three women of the Order of the Silver Compass pilot the three Paragon Warsuits represented in both preserved force snapshots.

One source-snapshot model is a Paragon Superior, but the project has not established which individual woman holds that role in narrative portrayal.

The trio is established collectively. The repository does not yet contain three established individual character identities.

## Established common ground with Fred

All three were present at **REMEMBER THE RETURN** with Fred, Constantia and Valeria.

They have a practiced operational relation with Valeria's Warglaive. Fred has directly observed enough of that coordination to begin understanding support, timing, commitment, recoverability and return through their interaction.

They also heard Fred's statement that he is watching how the group interacts with one another and the world so the force can win and return alive to the `True Meridian`.

See `../Relationships/RELATIONSHIPS.md#rel-fred-paragon-trio-knowledge` and `#rel-fred-current-group-command-intent`.

## Current continuity

Fred's later direct correction protects all three Paragon pilots as current-chamber participants.

The chamber still contains an unresolved one-person arithmetic conflict between Fred's exact nine-woman total and ten accumulated positive presence claims. Do not solve that conflict by denying any member of the trio.

## Other durable relations

The trio's practiced relation with Valeria's Warglaive is operational, not proof of friendship or common Knight/Sororitas ownership.

Constantia commands the trio through her authority over the Order of the Silver Compass. This does not establish special private familiarity or a unique command arrangement.

## Development rule

Differentiate the three women through repeated lived interaction, competence, role, choices, speech and accepted shared history.

Do not manufacture three personalities from adjectives, names or etymology merely to make bookkeeping easier.
