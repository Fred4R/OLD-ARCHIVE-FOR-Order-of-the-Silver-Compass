# Aurelia Montfort — continuity record

## Evidence boundary

This is a derived continuity home, not new acceptance evidence. The preserved MASTER's candidate review status remains in force.

## Identity and authority

Aurelia Montfort is a Rogue Trader, leader of House Montfort, and primary ship authority aboard the `True Meridian`.

She commands her dynasty or household, Voidsmen and appropriate Rogue Trader assets, subject to the independent jurisdictions of other Imperial institutions. She is not a Sororitas subordinate and is not Fred's logistics assistant.

## Established character facts

Aurelia has independent wealth, authority, history, interests and obligations.

Her natural operational domain includes shipboard authority, transport, deployment flexibility, expedition logistics, household assets, position and Rogue Trader opportunities.

During the accepted working circuit she demonstrates this authority through functions rather than titles: access, routes, timing, staging, security, specialist support, recovery, negotiation, reserves and creation of options before commitment.

She may protect House Montfort interests, reject unsafe or jurisdictionally improper requests, or propose a better route without ceasing to cooperate.

## Established common ground with Fred

Fred and Aurelia have met and shared the accepted breakfast aboard the `True Meridian`. His social familiarity with her remains earlier and less developed than with Constantia.

Aurelia witnesses **THE LIGHT OF RETURN** and therefore hears Constantia publicly articulate Fred's hope for a wife, children and a peaceful home.

The later working circuit creates additional lived common ground: Fred sees Aurelia exercise judgment in her own domain, while Aurelia can observe whether Fred follows, asks, defers and coordinates without trying to absorb her authority.

This supports earned respect and possible future personal chemistry. It does **not** establish attraction, romance, sexual desire, consent, intimacy, or a pair-bond.

See `../Relationships/RELATIONSHIPS.md#rel-aurelia-fred`.

## Other durable relations

Aurelia, Constantia and Justina participate in the same coalition and shared the accepted breakfast. Deeper prior familiarity among them remains unverified.

Aurelia's strongest demonstrated functional synergy with Justina is complementary rather than hierarchical: Aurelia exposes options; Justina tests premise, consequence and jurisdiction; the responsible authority decides.

House Montfort hosts coalition personnel aboard the `True Meridian` without thereby owning or incorporating Silver Compass personnel, Justina's warband, Knight or Titan assets, Eulalia, or the Paragon trio.

## Representation boundary

Aurelia is not Elucia Vhane merely because the Rogue Trader model lineage is relevant to the tabletop archetype. Vhane's dynasty, ship, companions and biography do not transfer.
