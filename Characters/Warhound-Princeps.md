# Project Warhound Princeps — continuity record

## Established identity and authority

A woman serves as Princeps of the project Warhound represented in `FORCE-3000`.

She commands the project Warhound. That does not subordinate the Silver Compass, House Montfort, Justina's Agents, Valeria, or Fred to her.

## Name state

Fred's later reported wording uses **Sabine**.

Earlier **Sabina** was a proposal and is not equally strong evidence of an accepted name. `Sabine` may be used as reported address, but formal naming acceptance remains unavailable.

Do not alternate spellings as though both were canon, and do not ask Fred to repair an assistant-created naming conflict.

Neither form establishes surname, Titan Legion, Warhound name, biography or personality.

## Established common ground with Fred

Fred has told the Princeps that senior responsibility includes guiding genuine subordinates while considering threats and consequences larger than the commander's own Warhound.

This establishes what she heard. It does not establish her agreement, private judgment, or a new chain of command.

See `../Relationships/RELATIONSHIPS.md#rel-fred-princeps-responsibility-statement`.

## Warhound relation

A Princeps is associated with and commands the project Warhound. The Princeps and Warhound are aboard the `True Meridian` in inherited continuity. Earlier continuation brought Fred's group to the Titan; the active scene later moved to Aurelia's working-and-receiving chamber.

See `../Relationships/RELATIONSHIPS.md#rel-princeps-warhound`.

## Current continuity

The Princeps is among the inherited positive presence claims in the current chamber. Do not identify her as the nine-versus-ten overcount without stronger evidence.
