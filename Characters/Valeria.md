# Valeria — continuity record

## Established identity and role

Valeria is the established pilot of the project Armiger Warglaive in the accepted **REMEMBER THE RETURN** continuity.

That is the durable identity currently supported. Do not expand it into a surname, noble title, Knight house, Freeblade status or Questor alignment without later accepted evidence.

## Established common ground with Fred

Fred and Valeria have met and interacted directly.

Valeria gives Fred a practical lesson using the Warglaive and its practiced operational relation with the three Silver Compass Paragon pilots. Fred begins to understand support, timing, commitment, recoverability and return through that interaction.

Valeria directly hears Fred say that he is watching how they interact with one another and the world so the force can win and return alive to the `True Meridian`.

This is an experiential learning and communication relation. It does not make Fred a Knight specialist and does not prove agreement on every future tactical choice.

See `../Relationships/RELATIONSHIPS.md#rel-valeria-fred`.

## Established functional relation

Valeria's Warglaive has a practiced operational relation with the three unnamed Silver Compass Paragon pilots. The relation is operational and historical; it does not by itself establish friendship, family, exact duration, common ownership or a cross-institutional chain of command.

See `../Relationships/RELATIONSHIPS.md#rel-valeria-paragon-trio`.
