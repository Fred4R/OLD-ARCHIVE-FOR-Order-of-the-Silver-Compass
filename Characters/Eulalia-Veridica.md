# Sister Dialogus Eulalia Veridica — continuity record

## Evidence boundary

This is the durable retrieval home for the Eulalia facts already carried by the project. It does not create new biography.

## Identity and role

Eulalia Veridica is a Sister Dialogus who operates with the Silver Compass force represented in both preserved snapshots.

Her personal name is established. She introduced herself as **Sister Dialogus Eulalia Veridica** after the accepted grounding exchange beneath the Warhound, so Fred and others in earshot may know her name from that point onward.

Her exact institutional Order Dialogus origin and formal arrangement with the Silver Compass remain unresolved.

## Established function and behavior

Eulalia can receive words or signals, distinguish witness from interpretation, ground shared meaning, recapitulate already-established intent, transmit clarified meaning where appropriate, and invite correction.

She demonstrated that function after Constantia's luminous declaration. She is not omniscient and does not gain hidden thoughts, divine intention or undisclosed evidence through rhetoric or faith.

Her name's semantic resonance does not make her infallible or dictate her personality.

## Established common ground with Fred

Eulalia hears Constantia publicly state Fred's domestic hope at **THE LIGHT OF RETURN**.

She recapitulates shared observations and Fred's command intent while distinguishing them from interpretation, explicitly invites correction, and then introduces herself by name.

The durable relational consequence is a minimal personal acquaintance grounded in communication and correction. This does **not** establish friendship, attraction, intimacy, special trust, or authority over Fred.

See `../Relationships/RELATIONSHIPS.md#rel-eulalia-fred`.

## Current continuity

Eulalia's continuous presence is protected by the current continuity record: no departure and return occurred.

Fred's direct total is exactly nine women. The former nine-versus-ten wording came from combining later protected corrections with an older candidate enumeration that the assistant had miscounted and that mixed people already present with people entering. Use `../CURRENT_STATE.md` for the corrected attendance-provenance boundary.

## Functional relations

In both stored snapshots the Dialogus supports Constantia's Retributor formation. That source-snapshot attachment does not establish personal familiarity or friendship with Constantia.

Eulalia operates with the Silver Compass force, but whether she is incorporated, seconded from an Order Dialogus, or connected by another arrangement remains unresolved.

Her presence aboard the `True Meridian` does not establish a permanent House Montfort communications office.
