# Justina Voss — continuity record

## Evidence boundary

This record extracts durable Justina continuity. It does not turn inherited project standing into newly witnessed acceptance.

## Identity and authority

Justina Voss is an Inquisitor with an independent Inquisitorial presence or warband. She retains the extraordinary independence and jurisdiction appropriate to an Inquisitor and directs her own Agents.

Coalition coordination does not subordinate her office to Fred, Constantia or Aurelia.

## Established character facts

Justina has a distinct organizational identity from the Sisters and House Montfort.

Her professional perspective includes enemy intent, investigation, hidden motives, suspicious behavior, unusual information and the capabilities of her Agents. She can make strong inferences without becoming omniscient.

During Aurelia's accepted working circuit she demonstrates a repeatable method for making ambiguity legible: distinguish what is observed, what is inferred, what remains unknown, whose jurisdiction governs the decision, and what she recommends.

She treats correction as compatible with competence. A revised conclusion that better fits evidence is stronger than an unrevised conclusion defended for status.

## Established common ground with Fred

Fred and Justina have met and shared the accepted breakfast. His social familiarity with her remains early or developing.

Justina witnesses **THE LIGHT OF RETURN** and therefore knows from public speech that Fred hopes for a wife, children and a peaceful home. She also witnesses Constantia's refusal to confuse an observed luminous event with complete metaphysical interpretation.

During Aurelia's working circuit Fred sees Justina choose calibrated inquiry over needless intimidation when circumstances allow. That can support trust in her judgment without establishing emotional softness or attraction.

Fred later compares her, from his own perspective, to a noir detective: observant, probing and difficult to fool, combined with elegant professional skill and Inquisitorial jurisdiction. This is Fred's analogy, not a genre script or independent personality diagnosis.

See `../Relationships/RELATIONSHIPS.md#rel-justina-fred`.

## Other durable relations

Justina directs the Inquisitorial Agents represented in both preserved force snapshots. Their current physical presence in the chamber is not separately established.

Justina and Aurelia have demonstrated useful functional complementarity without either becoming subordinate to the other.

Justina, Constantia and Aurelia shared the accepted breakfast, but deeper prior history among them remains pending audit.
