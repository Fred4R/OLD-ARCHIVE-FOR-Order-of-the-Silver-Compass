0E. TYPED RELATION ARCHITECTURE
-------------------------------

The word relation is used here in a disciplined sense: a connection between two records whose kind,
direction, evidence, and limits can be stated. Historically, relation developed from Latin relatio,
"a bringing back" or "report." That older sense is useful for this Compendium: a relation should report
how two things connect without pretending they are the same thing.

When adding or interpreting a relation, identify its type where confusion is possible:

- IDENTITY RELATION — X is Y. Use only when the records identify the same entity.
- REPRESENTATION RELATION — a miniature, datasheet, archetype, symbol, or record represents something else
  without becoming identical to it.
- INSTITUTIONAL RELATION — membership, office, jurisdiction, attachment, alliance, or service between people
  and organizations.
- FUNCTIONAL RELATION — one element supports, enables, protects, communicates with, transports, heals, or
  otherwise affects another in a defined task.
- HISTORICAL RELATION — descent, prior accepted encounter, succession, or other established change through time.
- EVIDENTIAL RELATION — a source supports a claim within a stated scope.
- KNOWLEDGE RELATION — a person knows, observes, remembers, is told, or reasonably infers something.
- SYMBOLIC RELATION — a literary, heraldic, etymological, or thematic resonance. Symbolic relation is not
  evidence of identity, biography, psychology, or institutional fact.
- TEMPORAL RELATION — a rule, source snapshot, or continuity state is earlier, later, current as of a date,
  superseded, or still active for a defined purpose.

RELATION SAFEGUARD:  
A true relation of one type does not automatically create another. Supporting a Canoness on the tabletop does
not prove personal friendship. Sharing a ship does not prove institutional merger. A model resembling an official
named character does not transfer biography. A suggestive etymology does not establish personality. A later rule
does not rewrite an earlier roster export.

UPDATE PATTERN:  
For a new durable relation, preserve five elements when useful: endpoints -> relation type -> evidence/status ->
scope -> non-implication. This pattern is intended to make the Compendium easier for both humans and retrieval
systems to interpret consistently.

0F. CONTROLLED SEMANTIC VOCABULARY
----------------------------------

PURPOSE:  
Use the narrowest term that accurately describes the fact. The vocabulary below is not ornamental etymology; it is a
semantic control layer intended to prevent a human or AI from treating related words as interchangeable when the project
depends on their distinction.

- CANON — from Greek kanon, a rule or standard. Here it means accepted durable project reality. Appearance in narration, proposal, inference, or a roster does not by itself create canon.
- CONTINUITY — from Latin continuare, to join together or make continuous. Here it means the accepted temporal connection among events and states: what happened, what changed, and what remains true afterward.
- AUTHORITY — legitimate right to decide or direct within a defined scope. Authority is not identical to expertise, influence, seniority, or moral worth.
- JURISDICTION — from Latin ius (law/right) + dicere (to speak or declare). Here it means the domain within which an office or institution may legitimately exercise authority.
- COMMAND — the legitimate power to direct a subordinate force or formation. Command should not be used as a vague synonym for leadership, advice, or coordination.
- COORDINATION — literally arranging together: distinct actors or institutions align effort toward a shared objective while retaining their own command and jurisdiction unless a source explicitly establishes otherwise.
- SUPPORT — an enabling contribution to another actor or formation. Support does not by itself imply ownership, subordination, friendship, or transfer of authority.
- REPRESENTATION — making something present through a stand-in. A miniature, datasheet, symbol, or record may represent a fictional entity without being identical to every official exemplar associated with that model.
- WAYMARK — a retrievable narrative checkpoint marking where continuation should resume. A waymark preserves orientation; its contents become durable canon only to the extent explicitly accepted and incorporated into the Compendium.
- COMMAND INTENT — the desired purpose and end condition communicated by a commander so competent subordinates can adapt their actions without requiring every act to be prescribed. Here it states what Fred wants the expedition to accomplish across institutional boundaries; it does not transfer another leader's command or jurisdiction to him.
- PRINCEPS — Latin princeps, “first” or “chief.” In Titan service, the title identifies the person who commands a Titan. In this project, the Warhound's Princeps is an inherited role/person; Section 1 controls presence and Section 6.9 controls naming.
- ONOMASTICS — from Greek onoma / onyma, “name.” Onomastics is the study of names. In this Compendium, onomastic state records whether a person or entity has an established name, only a role-based identifier, or a provisional/unresolved proposed name. Etymology may guide semantic resonance, but a name's historical meaning does not determine the bearer's personality, destiny, morality, or institutional affiliation.
- RETINUE — from the idea of persons retained in another's service. In prose it may describe the people attending an Inquisitor, but the source-snapshot unit remains Inquisitorial Agents; using “retinue” does not assign personal names, ranks, or histories to those models.
- ENTOURAGE — French entourage, “surroundings” or those who surround, from entourer, “to surround.” In this project Rogue Trader Entourage names Aurelia's four-model specialist formation, not every person in House Montfort or aboard the True Meridian.
- EXPEDITION — from Latin expedire, “to free the feet, disentangle, make ready.” Here an expedition is an organized undertaking prepared to move beyond a secure base. The etymology is useful because preparation and freedom of movement are part of Aurelia's contribution; it does not imply haste or sole command.
- POSITIONING — from Latin positio, “placing.” In tactical language it means arranging people or units so later choices improve. Positioning is not itself victory, but it can preserve options and reduce the cost of achieving victory.
- RESERVE — from Latin reservare, “to keep back.” A reserve preserves a force or option for later commitment. In tabletop rules Strategic Reserves is a defined mechanical term and should not be generalized into fiction without context.
- WARRANT — a formal authorization or guarantee of authority. In Rogue Trader context, a Warrant of Trade is a defining Imperial instrument that licenses extraordinary action; the project's exact Montfort Warrant text, provenance, privileges, and limitations remain unresolved.
- WORKING CIRCUIT — project term for a purposeful walk through multiple operational functions of the True Meridian. It is not a formal Imperial naval term and does not establish a canonical deck plan.
- INQUISITION / INQUIRE — from Latin inquirere, “to seek into, investigate, ask after.” In this project the etymology supports Justina's disciplined search for what is true; it does not imply that every suspicion is correct.
- DISCERN — from Latin discernere, “to separate, distinguish, sift apart.” Justina's best use of precise diction is often discriminative: separate observation from inference, danger from uncertainty, and authority from preference.
- ECONOMY — from Greek oikonomia, “household management” or stewardship of a household. For House Montfort this is a useful older sense: Aurelia's competence includes arranging people, stores, access, movement, and opportunity, not merely accumulating wealth.
- DISPOSITION — from Latin disponere, “to arrange, set in order.” In tactical usage, disposition is the arrangement of forces and positions before or during commitment.
- PRUDENCE — from Latin prudentia, practical foresight and judgment. Here it means competent judgment about consequences, not timidity or refusal to act.
- VICTORY — from Latin victoria, success in overcoming an opponent or contest. In the tactical project, victory means accomplishing the game's or operation's winning condition; merely minimizing casualties is not victory.
- PRESERVATION — from Latin praeservare, to guard beforehand. Here it means deliberately avoiding unnecessary loss of people, capability, or future options. Preservation is a preference among viable winning lines, not a prohibition on necessary risk.
- SURVIVAL — from Latin supervivere, to live beyond. Here it concerns persons remaining alive through the commitment. It is a valued outcome but does not redefine a failed mission as success.
- RETURN — from Old French retorner, to turn back. Here it means actual restoration of people or forces to a safer or homeward state after commitment; it is an outcome, not a synonym for retreat.
- RECOVERABILITY — the retained capacity to disengage, be supported, reorganize, and return after commitment. A force can be recoverable before it has actually returned.
- GUIDANCE — direction that helps another person orient judgment or action. Guidance may occur inside or outside a command relationship; where formal subordination exists, guidance does not replace the more exact term command for orders.
- RESPONSIBILITY — from the family of Latin respondere, “to answer.” Here it means answerability for decisions, duties, and foreseeable consequences within a person's actual role and authority. Responsibility should not be expanded beyond jurisdiction merely because a person is senior.
- SCALE — the magnitude or level at which a problem is considered. Increasing scale can change relationships, constraints, support needs, and consequences; it should not be treated as merely the same problem with larger numbers.
- MIRACLE — from Latin miraculum, a wonder or object of wonder, from mirari, “to wonder.” In this Compendium the word may first record a witnessed extraordinary event. A stronger theological claim about divine cause or message requires separate evidence or accepted interpretation.
- HOME — from Old English ham, a dwelling or place of belonging. In Fred's present statement, home means the hoped-for domestic place shared with wife and children; it is not interchangeable with the True Meridian, a command post, or an institution.
- FAMILY — historically related to Latin familia, whose range could encompass a household more broadly than modern nuclear kin. In this continuity Fred has supplied the narrower intended meaning: wife and children. Do not enlarge or dilute that meaning through etymology.
- INTIMACY — from Latin intimus, “inmost.” Here it means increasing access to another person's private thoughts, vulnerabilities, ordinary life, or embodied presence. Intimacy can be professional, emotional, spiritual, romantic, or sexual; one type does not prove another.
- SENSUALITY — from Late Latin sensualis, from sensus, “sense” or “feeling.” Here it means narration that attends to bodily and sensory presence: voice, breath, warmth, scent, gaze, distance, movement, fabric, armour, skin, and touch where appropriate. Sensuality is not a synonym for sexual consent or sexual availability.
- ATTRACTION — from the Latin family of attrahere, “to draw toward.” Here it means person-specific interest or pull. A glance, proximity, beauty, or admiration may contribute evidence but does not by itself establish reciprocal romantic or sexual attraction.
- SEXUAL / ROMANTIC UNDERTONE — a narrative register in which adult characters may become aware of sex, attraction, desirability, or romantic possibility without explicit sexual action. Undertone is not outcome: it does not establish consent, exclusivity, pair-bonding, or a future relationship.

- DIALOGUE — from Greek dialogos, formed from dia (“through” or “across”) and logos (“word,” “speech,” “account,” or “reason”). It does not etymologically mean “two people talking.” In this project, dialogue is meaning worked out through exchange.
- COMMON GROUND — information that participants mutually treat as available for the present interaction. It is not identical to objective truth: people can share an understanding that is incomplete or mistaken, so the Compendium separately tracks evidence and accuracy.
- GROUNDING — the collaborative process of establishing that a contribution has been understood well enough for the current purpose. Here it is a communication concept used to preserve continuity and shared meaning; it is not an official Sororitas doctrine.
- SYNERGY — from Greek synergia, “working together.” Here it means that distinct competencies reinforce one another toward a shared end without requiring institutional merger, duplicated expertise, or loss of jurisdiction.
- RECAPITULATION — from Latin recapitulare, “to go again through the main heads or points.” A recapitulation compresses established meaning for retrieval; it must not introduce new facts while pretending merely to summarize them.

LEXICAL RULE:  
When two terms above could both sound plausible, prefer the one that states the narrower relation. For example, Fred
coordinates a coalition objective; Constantia commands the Order of the Silver Compass. A Paragon trio may
support a Warglaive without becoming subordinate to its pilot. Preservation keeps lives and options from being
wasted; recoverability keeps a path back available; return is the realized movement or restoration homeward;
survival describes who remains alive; and victory remains the primary tactical outcome.

2.1 THE ADEPTA SORORITAS — OFFICIAL CONTEXT
-------------------------------------------


STATUS: INHERITED_LORE_CLAIM

The Adepta Sororitas are a martial sisterhood of elite human warriors devoted to the God-Emperor.
They are not genetically enhanced Space Marines. Their Orders Militant form the military arm commonly
known as the Sisters of Battle, while other Sororitas traditions include non-militant Orders such as
the Orders Hospitaller and Orders Pronatus.

Their institutional origin lies in the Age of Apostasy. The Brides of the Emperor served Goge Vandire
until Alicia Dominica and her companions turned against him after being brought before the Golden
Throne. The Adepta Sororitas were subsequently reformed as a nominally independent institution with
close ties to the Ecclesiarchy.

The six Orders Majoris grew around Alicia Dominica and her five companions, but official material also
states that uncounted lesser Orders exist. This provides an official-lore-compatible category within
which a custom daughter Order such as the Order of the Silver Compass can exist without becoming
official Games Workshop canon.

Battle Sisters commonly come from the Schola Progenium and undergo intense training and discipline.
Official material repeatedly emphasizes the Sororitas' "Holy Trinity" of bolt, flame, and melta
weaponry. Their faith is not merely decorative culture in the setting: official Warhammer material
describes Battle Sisters as performing genuine miracles through their devotion to the Emperor.

Official Warhammer material also describes the Adepta Sororitas as accompanying Imperial expeditions
ranging from pilgrim processions to major crusade fleets.

The Battle Sisters of the Orders Militant form the principal fighting bodies represented by most of
the Sororitas infantry in these lists.

SOURCE: OFF-SOB-01; OFF-SOB-11.

2.2 THE ORDER OF THE SACRED ROSE — OFFICIAL CONTEXT
---------------------------------------------------


STATUS: INHERITED_LORE_CLAIM

The Order of the Sacred Rose is one of the six Orders Majoris of the Adepta Sororitas.
Official Warhammer material published on 25 February 2026 states that the Bloody Rose and Sacred Rose
were the final two Orders Majoris, founded in the 38th Millennium.

The Sacred Rose marks its holy center at the Convent Prioris on Terra and honors Saint Arabella,
one of Alicia Dominica's original companions. Arabella is remembered as level-headed and serene,
with a sacred role associated with liberation of the oppressed.

Sacred Rose Sisters are described as notably calm and unflinching in battle, favouring prayers of
fortitude and strength rather than bloodthirsty war cries.

SOURCE: OFF-SR-01.

CANON SAFEGUARD:
The Order of the Silver Compass descends from the Sacred Rose, but ancestry does NOT automatically
make every Sacred Rose custom, personality trait, doctrine, or historical event identical in the
daughter Order. Only explicitly established Silver Compass traits are Silver Compass canon.

2.5 ADEPTA SORORITAS ROLE CONNECTIONS — OFFICIAL CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------

STATUS: INHERITED_LORE_CLAIM WITH EXPLICIT LIMITS

The Adepta Sororitas unite several religious and practical functions without making those functions identical.
For this project, the distinctions below are especially important:

- Orders Militant provide the principal Battle Sister fighting formations.
- Orders Dialogus are associated with sacred speech and the interpretation of communications; current
  Novitiate material specifically shows cryptographic training, cipher-breaking, hidden-pattern recognition,
  stirring rhetoric, and use of an auto-broadcaster.
- Dogmata are spiritual exemplars and disciplinarians within Sororitas military culture. They lead war hymns
  and possess grave authority to judge and condemn Sisters. A Dogmata is therefore not interchangeable with a Dialogus.
- Orders Hospitaller tend physical and spiritual wounds, including under battlefield conditions.
- Orders Pronatus recover, protect, and restore sacred relics. Novitiate Reliquarius material shows the wider
  Sororitas practice of carrying inspiring icons, saintly remains, and simulacra of famous artefacts.
- Imagifier / Simulacrum-bearing roles belong to the same broader culture of visible sacred representation,
  but this Compendium does not infer an Imagifier's institutional origin from Orders Pronatus material without a
  direct official source.
- Exorcists make the union of liturgy and warfare unusually literal: official material depicts the vehicle as
  a mobile pipe-organ missile platform accompanied by hymnals.
- Paragon Warsuit pilots are officially described as training to fight as trios, making coordination among
  three pilots an official unit-level precedent without establishing any project's pilot names or relationships.
- Celestian Insidiant Denuncia provide a current official example of cross-role learning: the Denuncia
  has studied with the Orders Dialogus and is a practiced orator whose voice is used as a weapon against witches.
  This establishes that Dialogus training can inform another Sororitas combat role without making that combatant
  a Dialogus or making every Dialogus an anti-psyker specialist.

INTERPRETATION RULE:  
Connection is not equivalence. Similar religious functions may cooperate or overlap without becoming the same
office, Order, or chain of authority.

PROJECT RELEVANCE:  
This map is intended to prevent future narrative from flattening the Sororitas into one generic priesthood. It
provides a lawful bridge for distinct characters to reveal different aspects of the same faith while retaining
their own institutional competence.

SOURCES: OFF-SOB-01; OFF-SOB-03; OFF-SOB-04; OFF-SOB-08; OFF-SOB-09; OFF-SOB-10; OFF-SOB-INSIDIANTS-01.


2.6 OFFICIAL NAMING PRECEDENTS — DESCRIPTIVE, NOT PRESCRIPTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------

STATUS: INHERITED_LORE_CLAIM WITH ONOMASTIC LIMITS  
PREDECESSOR CLAIMED CHECK DATE: 2026-09-11.

Official Warhammer material supports a naming architecture, but not a single universal naming language for Imperial
characters. The examples below are therefore precedents, not templates to be copied mechanically.

- Sacred Rose / Adepta Sororitas: current official Sacred Rose material names Sister Superior Lyona Serena;
  official Black Library promotion also identifies Aveline as a Sister of the Sacred Rose. Broader official Sororitas
  material includes names such as Erika Luminas, Aestred Thurga, Agathae Dolan, Junith Eruita, and
  Morvenn Vahl. These examples are linguistically varied. They do not support a rule that all Sisters must bear Latin
  names, even though High Gothic, Latin-derived, Romance, Germanic, and invented forms are all compatible with the setting.
- Orders Dialogus: the Order of the Resounding Vow is an official institutional name. It demonstrates that an Order
  title can be semantically transparent and devotional while remaining separate from an individual Dialogus's personal name.
- Imperial Knights / Armigers: current official Combat Patrol material distinguishes pilot and engine explicitly:
  Pilot Dantos pilots the Armiger Helverin Thauvir, while Pilot Morrigan pilots the Armiger Warglaive Viridor.
  The same source describes Morrigan as having risen from lowly retainer to bondsman through service and bravery. Other
  official Knight material likewise permits both noble and lowborn Armiger pilots. Therefore a pilot's personal name,
  hereditary title, household affiliation, and engine-name are separate fields and must not be inferred from one another.
- Titan Legions: official Warhammer material distinguishes Princeps Senioris Areum Keeso, the Warlord Titan
  Clavius Dread, and Legio Tempestus as three different identity layers; it also names Princeps Gemnon separately
  from the Titan Aurora Ferrum. This supports a project architecture of person -> office -> engine -> Legio rather
  than treating “Princeps” or the Titan's proper name as the operator's personal name. These 31st-millennium exemplars do
  not establish one mandatory 41st-millennium naming language.
- Inquisitorial Agents: current official Kill Team material describes Inquisitorial teams as drawing agents from all
  strata of Imperial society and distinguishes specialist roles such as Interrogator, Autosavant, Questkeeper, Death World
  Veteran, Enlightener, Hexorcist, Mystic, Pistolier, and Gun Servitor. This breadth argues against imposing one shared
  ethnic, linguistic, or aristocratic naming pattern on Justina's individual Agents before their origins are established.

ONOMASTIC INTERPRETATION RULE:
Use official naming examples to establish what is permitted and what identity layers must remain separate. Then verify the
historical etymology of any proposed project name independently. Choose names for semantic resonance, phonetic distinction,
and institutional fit; do not reverse the process by inventing a biography merely because a name happens to mean “victory,”
“peace,” “watchful,” “steadfast,” or another attractive concept.

SOURCES: OFF-SR-01; OFF-SR-AVELINE-01; OFF-SOB-08; OFF-SOB-NAMING-01; OFF-IK-ARMIGER-CP-01;
OFF-IK-CRUSADE-01; OFF-TITAN-PRINCEPS-01; OFF-IA-02.

OFFICIAL ADJACENT RULES EVIDENCE — KILL TEAM, NOT MAIN-GAME 11TH EDITION:
Current Novitiate Dialogus rules include Stirring Rhetoric, which supports another Novitiate, and an

rhetoric and broadcast technology are official parts of the Dialogus training image. They are not the current
Warhammer 40,000 Dialogus datasheet rules and must not be imported mechanically into the main game.

OFFICIAL MODEL-VISUAL ANCHOR:
Official Games Workshop product imagery for the 2020 Dialogus sculpt visibly depicts:
- an adult Sister with her face exposed and framed by a high hood/gorget;
- her mouth open in forceful proclamation;
- a tall staff of office with fleur-de-lis imagery and attached parchment;
- extensive written text, scrolls, and devotional parchment around the figure;
- a lectern-like frontal assembly with candles and hanging text;
- a book-and-reliquary-like apparatus rising behind the head, including skull and candle imagery.

These are sculpt-visible features. Studio paint colours, facial ethnicity, hair beneath the hood,
exact age, body measurements, and private personality are not established by the sculpt alone.

PROJECT IDENTITY:
CHAR-EULALIA — Sister Dialogus Eulalia Veridica.

She supports Constantia's Retributor formation in both stored snapshots and is physically present in the current True Meridian scene.
Her exact Order Dialogus, convent of origin, duration of service with the Order of the Silver Compass, relationship with Constantia
before the currently established events, and permanent shipboard role aboard the True Meridian remain UNRESOLVED.

ONOMASTIC RATIONALE — PROJECT, NOT OFFICIAL LORE:
- Eulalia derives from Ancient Greek eulalos, “sweetly/well-speaking,” from eu, “well,” and laleō/lalia, “to speak/speech.”
- Veridica uses the feminine Latin form of veridicus, “truth-telling, veracious,” from verus, “true,” and dicere/dico, “to say.”
- The combined semantic resonance — well-spoken + truth-telling — fits the project's communication/grounding function and the official
  Dialogus associations with cryptography, rhetoric, hymns, and communications.
- The name does not imply infallibility, ancestry, a particular ethnicity, predetermined temperament, sanctity beyond her office,
  romantic destiny, or a Games Workshop-authored High Gothic translation. Official Warhammer material treats High Gothic as an
  in-universe language rather than literal real-world Latin; Latin and Greek etymology here is an external naming design tool.

PROJECT PORTRAYAL BRIDGE — PROVISIONAL, NOT CANON:
For future narrative, preserve the official sculpt's central visual idea for Eulalia: a woman whose face, mouth,
voice, written texts, staff, and devotional apparatus make communication physically visible.

A project-specific sensual portrayal should therefore emphasize embodied communication rather than
inventing revealing clothing or treating attractiveness as an official trait. The most natural points
of attention are:
- the exposed face within the severe religious frame of the hood;
- eyes that visibly track a listener or a line of text;
- lips, jaw, throat, and breath involved in projection;
- the grip and fine movement of her hands around staff, parchment, stylus, or lectern;
- the contrast between disciplined posture and the movement of layered cloth as she walks;
- the weight and nearness of books, wax, parchment, metal, incense, and vox machinery around her.

If a later story establishes complexion, hair, eye colour, stature, build, age, scars, voice quality,
or other intimate visual details, treat each as PROJECT_CHARACTER_DESIGN until Fred accepts it as a
durable character fact.

BEHAVIORAL PREMISE — PROJECT INFERENCE FROM OFFICIAL ROLE:
The strongest lore-compatible behavioral pattern is not "she always knows the truth." It is:
receive -> distinguish -> decipher -> recite or communicate -> check whether meaning survived.

That pattern is a project inference from official hymn-leading and cryptographic training. It is useful
for portraying competence, but it must not be represented as a quoted Games Workshop job description.

PROJECT CONTINUATION FUNCTION — USER-DIRECTED; NOT AN OFFICIAL JOB DESCRIPTION:  
Fred has directed that the Dialogus should seamlessly support the project's intended purposes. The accepted portrayal rule is therefore:

receive -> distinguish -> ground -> recapitulate -> transmit -> invite correction.

- receive: attend to the actual words, signal, text, hymn, or witnessed event;
- distinguish: separate observation, quotation, inference, interpretation, uncertainty, and private state;
- ground: help participants establish that the meaning is understood well enough for the present purpose;
- recapitulate: compress already-established meaning without smuggling in new facts;
- transmit: carry that clarified meaning across role or institutional boundaries where appropriate;
- invite correction: remain corrigible; if Constantia, Aurelia, Justina, Fred, or another competent participant says the recapitulation is inaccurate, the wording is revised rather than defended for prestige.

This pattern gives the project a natural in-world mechanism for context refresh. It also fits the project's deeper theme: competence builds trust when people can tell the difference between what is known, what is meant, and what is merely assumed.

SCOPE LIMIT:  
The Dialogus does not become omniscient, a human database, the narrator's surrogate, or the final arbiter of meaning. She cannot know private attraction, undisclosed motives, hidden evidence, or the Emperor's intentions merely because she is skilled with language. Her competence is strongest when there is something communicable to receive, compare, decipher, recite, or clarify.

LEXICAL / THEOLOGICAL PORTRAYAL NOTE — PROJECT INFERENCE, NOT OFFICIAL DOCTRINE:
The words voyeur and exhibition should not be treated as simple synonyms for seeing and showing. Voyeur entered
English from French voyeur, literally a viewer or one who sees, but its modern ordinary and sexual senses add intrusive,
indiscreet, or sexually gratified observation of another person's private or intimate activity. Exhibition derives through
Latin exhibere, to present or hold forth for view; the broad word can name any deliberate display, while sexual
exhibitionism is a much narrower concept involving eroticized exposure or display.

A lore-compatible portrayal of this Dialogus may therefore have her distinguish witness, observer, spectator,
voyeur, display, and sexual exhibition according to intention, privacy, audience, consent, and communicative
purpose. Her piety and training make that semantic discipline plausible; they do not establish a universal Sororitas sexual
ethic, a vow of celibacy, or this individual's private erotic preferences. If later narrative gives her a personal theological
position, preserve it as PROJECT_CHARACTER_DESIGN or accepted character canon only after Fred accepts it.

STORY-STRUCTURE GUIDANCE — NON-CANONICAL:
When the Dialogus appears in a scene:
1. begin with a concrete message, text, hymn, code, or spoken ambiguity;
2. let her physically handle or listen to it;
3. show what she can verify from the material;
4. preserve what she cannot verify;
5. let her communicate the result to another person whose response matters;
6. allow personality, attraction, humor, faith, or disagreement to emerge through that exchange rather
   than from an explanatory biography.

This makes her official lore function causally relevant while leaving room for a distinct project person
to emerge over repeated encounters.

INTERPRETATION SAFEGUARD:
The Dialogus is a particularly apt literary symbol for receiving, deciphering, comparing, recording, and
communicating information, but official Warhammer lore does not assign Dialogus personnel the specific
function of maintaining this project's Compendium. A side story may use that resonance without turning
metaphor into official lore or durable project canon.

INSTITUTIONAL SAFEGUARD:
Her presence in a Silver Compass army list does not by itself prove that she belongs institutionally to
the Order of the Silver Compass's Order Militant. Official lore supports distinct Orders Dialogus. Her
actual project origin and terms of attachment remain unresolved until established.

ANCESTRY / LITURGY BOUNDARY:
The Silver Compass descends from the Sacred Rose, whose official tradition includes calm hymns of hope,
salvation, fortitude, and strength. That ancestry makes a restrained or hopeful Silver Compass liturgical
style plausible. It does not establish this Dialogus's personal Order of origin, speaking style, emotional
temperament, or exact devotional repertoire. Those remain project characterization until accepted.

6. NAMED CHARACTERS

CHARACTER RECORD PATTERN:
Each major character record separates:
1. established character/world facts;
2. tabletop and model representation;
3. portrayal constraints;
4. established shared history/common ground;
5. unresolved or emergent material.

REPRESENTATION SAFEGUARD:
Keep five layers separate whenever they exist:
1. physical miniature or sculpt — the actual model or kit used, if known;
2. current datasheet — the rules identity used in Warhammer 40,000;
3. official archetype — the generic institution or role represented by that datasheet;
4. official named exemplar — a named Games Workshop character tied to a sculpt, kit, Combat Patrol, or source, when relevant;
5. project identity — the Order of the Silver Compass character.

Similarity between layers does not transfer biography, institutional history, companion names, ship names, heraldry, personality, or relationships unless the project explicitly establishes them.

A portrayal constraint is not an event.
A project fact is not automatically something Fred personally knows.
A plausible relationship outcome is not established history.

CANON SAFEGUARD:
A new appearance, habit, preference, biography detail, attraction cue, or other personal detail introduced in active narration remains scene-local working material until it is explicitly accepted or incorporated by an audited later revision. Do not infer physical appearance or personality from a person's name, etymology, institutional affiliation, heraldry, or literary symbolism.

INTERPRETATION RULE:
Keep world fact, character knowledge, and narrator inference distinct. A character may know something because they perceived it, were told it, remember it from accepted history, or can reasonably infer it from available evidence. Narrator access does not automatically become that character's knowledge.

MODEL-ANCHORED PORTRAYAL BRIDGE:
When a tabletop model without a fully established project character becomes important in narrative, build the portrayal in layers:

1. official institutional anchor — what Games Workshop lore establishes about the role or Order;
2. official model-visual anchor — what the physical sculpt visibly depicts;
3. current rules anchor — the model's present tabletop identity and role, dated when currentness matters;
4. project design space — appearance, habits, voice, sensuality, biography, and relationships not supplied by official evidence;
5. accepted durable character fact — only what Fred explicitly accepts or a later audited Compendium revision incorporates.

Use the official sculpt to preserve recognizable silhouette, regalia, and material culture, but do not treat studio paint, facial ethnicity, body proportions, hair, age, sexuality, temperament, or private biography as official lore unless an official source actually establishes them.

SENSORY PORTRAYAL RULE:
Sensual description may attend closely to an adult character's face, eyes, mouth, voice, breath, posture, hands, motion, clothing, and physical presence without turning attraction into reciprocal knowledge or official lore. Prefer embodied observation over anatomical inventory. If a detail is newly invented for the project, mark it as provisional until accepted.

ETYMOLOGICAL DICTION RULE:
Use etymology to sharpen distinctions, not to dictate character. A word's historical roots may illuminate
a motif such as dialogue, relation, tradition, symbol, or comprehension, but linguistic ancestry is not
evidence of a person's appearance, psychology, fate, or in-universe naming intention unless the project
explicitly establishes that intention.
