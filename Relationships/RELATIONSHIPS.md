# Durable relationships

## Evidence boundary

This registry extracts durable relationship knowledge so future conversations do not have to reconstruct it from the full MASTER.

It is not a new source of acceptance. The preserved MASTER remains a **REVISED CANDIDATE — user acceptance not established**. Where a relation below carries inherited `ESTABLISHED_PROJECT_CANON` standing, that standing remains subject to the source's evidentiary limits.

A relationship record must separate:
- observable interaction;
- what each person can actually know;
- the durable relational consequence;
- what remains unresolved.

Functional cooperation is not automatically friendship. Shared witness is not attraction. Attraction is not consent. Intimacy, desire, commitment and partnership are separate states.

## Relational truth rule

Record observable cues before assigning private motive.

A look can be established as a look without becoming proof of attraction. Reciprocal attraction requires evidence from both sides. Shared vulnerability can deepen common ground without creating romance. Cooperation can build trust without creating friendship.

The project may use embodied or mildly sensual description among adults, but narrative register is not an outcome generator. Constantia's luminous event is not sexual in itself, and acknowledging embodied attraction does not establish a supernatural message.

Functional relations must not be promoted into personal relationships without accepted interpersonal evidence.
